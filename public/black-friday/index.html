What SnowWall Pro includes

  • All 12 effects Blizzard, Rain on Glass, Christmas, Cherry Blossom, Your Emoji and more.
  • Ambient Mode Takes over the screen when you step away, with a clock over the effect.
  • Visual enhancements Bloom, crystalline sparkle, aurora, depth of field and four more.
  • Unlimited presets Save as many custom presets as you like. Free includes 3.
  • Ultra quality Up to 10,000 particles at your display’s full refresh rate.

Snowfall and Rain stay free, with every setting, whether you buy Pro or not.

How to buy

  1. Get SnowWall from the Mac App Store. The app itself is free.
  2. Click the snowflake in the menu bar and choose SnowWall Pro…, or click SnowWall Pro at the top of the Settings window.
  3. Buy it once. It is not a subscription, and you can restore it on every Mac signed in to your Apple Account.

Christmas is free in December. The Christmas effect is open to everyone for the whole month.
