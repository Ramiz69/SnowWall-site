Questions.

Is SnowWall free?

Yes. Snowfall and Rain are free, with every setting, and you can save three presets. SnowWall Pro is a one-time purchase that adds the other eight effects, Ambient Mode, the visual effects, Ultra quality and unlimited presets. It is not a subscription.

I opened SnowWall. Where is it?

SnowWall has no window or Dock icon of its own. It lives in the menu bar as a snowflake at the top of the screen. Click it to pause, switch effects or open Settings.

Will it slow my Mac down or drain the battery?

It draws on the graphics processor, so it uses some energy. It pauses when the display sleeps and drops to 30 frames a second in Low Power Mode or when the Mac runs hot. For less, choose Battery Saver quality or lower the snowflake count.

Does it need any permissions?

No. SnowWall draws over the desktop without reading what is on screen. If macOS ever asks you to give it screen recording or accessibility access, don’t grant it, and please let us know.

Can the snow stay behind my windows?

Yes. Turn on Keep Effects Behind Windows in Settings, under Interaction. The snow then falls between your wallpaper and your desktop icons.

I use Reduce Motion.

When Reduce Motion is on, SnowWall stops the snow by default. You can choose to slow it down instead, or to leave it running.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Can I pause it without the menu?

Yes. Add SnowWall’s control to Control Center, or use the Pause action in Spotlight or Shortcuts.
